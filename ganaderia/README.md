# Ganadería en perspectiva — Santa Cruz

Reviewed extract of the supplied Data Aike archive (19 November 2026). Source: SENASA / SAGyP, as documented in that archive.

The departmental bovine table covers 2008–2019. The missing Deseado observation in 2009 was corrected from zero to blank. Do not treat partial provincial totals as complete.

The original archive's figure generator was successfully executed during review. It reported 85,817 bovines in 2008 and 106,670 in 2019; Río Chico increased from 16,901 to 28,004 and Güer Aike declined from 34,140 to 22,674. These are stock observations, not annual production. Reference dates differ between bovines and sheep.
